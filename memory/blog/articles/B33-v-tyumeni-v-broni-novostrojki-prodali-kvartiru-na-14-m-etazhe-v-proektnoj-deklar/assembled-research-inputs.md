## Legal / normative (consultant.ru, accessed 2026-09-24)

### 214-FZ

- **Art. 19 p.1** — project declaration is official document with legal consequences for developer.
- **Art. 19 p.4** — monthly updates by 10th of next month via EISZhS.
- **Art. 19 p.7** — participant may sue to void deal as made under mistake if declaration requirements violated.
- **Art. 19 p.8** — liability for incomplete/unreliable declaration info (except technical errors).
- **Art. 21 p.5** — declaration must contain location and **characteristics** of buildings under construction.
- **Art. 21 p.2** — on request, developer must provide project documentation including changes.
- **Art. 4 p.4(1)** — DDU must identify object with floor, plan; includes **number of floors** of building among main characteristics.
- **Art. 4 p.4.1** — essential DDU terms must **match declaration at signing**; violation → court may void on participant's claim.

### Minstroy form (Order 239/пр, consultant LAW_417299)

- **Section 9.2.19–9.2.20** — min and max floors in object.
- **Section 9.4.1–9.4.3** — counts of passenger, cargo (≥1000 kg), cargo-passenger lifts.
- **No mandatory field** «floors served by each lift» — only aggregate counts.

### SP 54.13330.2022 (proekt-kom.ru mirror, accessed 2026-09-24)

- **9.16** — lifts required when top residential floor is **>12 m** above entrance lobby floor.
- **9.18** — in buildings **with** lift, **may omit lift stop on overbuilt floor** (context: one-floor extension of 5-storey buildings); also App. V note: for multi-level top apartments, stop may be on one level of apartment; building height for lift count = upper stop floor.
- **9.19** — if wheelchair-accessible flats above 1st floor → lifts or platforms per SP 59.13330.
- App. V (Garant) — **13–19 floors** → minimum **2** passenger lifts (table; verify exact row in Writer only as norm reference).

## Practical mechanism for Writer (facts only)

- **Declaration gap:** buyer sees «14 floors in building» + «N lifts» but must use **project docs / floor plans / lift scheme** (developer must disclose per art. 21) to learn **last served floor**.
