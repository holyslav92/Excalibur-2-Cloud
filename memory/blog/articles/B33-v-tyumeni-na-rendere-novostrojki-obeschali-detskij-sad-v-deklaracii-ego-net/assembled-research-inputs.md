## Legal / official framework (consultant.ru + Minstroy form; accessed 2026-09-24)

### Ст. 19 214-ФЗ — проектная декларация
- Official document; defines scope of developer’s right to attract funds (ч. 1).
- Changes **monthly by the 10th** via ЕИСЖС (ч. 4).
- Participant may seek invalidation of deal as **mistake** if declaration requirements violated (ч. 7).
- Developer **liable** for incomplete/unreliable information in declaration and in public materials where law requires disclosure (ч. 8).
URL: https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_51038/a9d2c9fd3a6b153a7b2094a93824ee93f1ed2881/

### Ст. 21 214-ФЗ — состав сведений о проекте
- Declaration must disclose project construction information, including rights to land, engineering connection, **planned transfer terms**, etc.
- On request, developer must provide documents confirming rights to land (st. 21 in conjunction with st. 19).
URL: https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_51038/aa003239194cdccc51aac0d1eae29e47362cac58/

### Приказ Минстроя № 239/пр (форма декларации) — **Раздел 22**
- **«Информация об объекте социальной инфраструктуры»**, when costs of building/reconstructing such object are reimbursed **fully or partly from DDU participants’ money**.
- Fields include **22.1** — type and purpose of social infrastructure object; **agreements** with state/municipality on **transfer** of social infrastructure to public ownership (as referenced in st. 18.1 214-FZ in form text).
- **If kindergarten is not listed in section 22** (or section not filled for DOU), the **legal obligation to build/transfer that DOU at participants’ expense** is not established by declaration the same way as for listed objects.
- Consultant fragment of form section 22: https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_210203/e67b4489e31e8fadbca9191162925044d57f85b3/

### Practical distinction for Writer (from form logic + casus)
- **Children’s playground** on courtyard (благоустройство / элемент двора) **≠** standalone **municipal kindergarten (ДОУ)** with capacity, transfer agreement, and section 22 cost recovery.
- **Marketing render** of a building «детский сад» + oral/booking note **≠** section 22 entry.
- **Future city/municipal DOU** (72.ru, «Лира», GPP «Иванушka») may be **real** but **outside** one developer’s declaration — supports manager’s «city later» without making render a guarantee.

### ДДU vs declaration
- Essential terms of DDU must align with **published declaration**; check **object address, stage/corpus, price, transfer term**, and **attachments** describing infrastructure **before** signing and **before** escrow.
- Booking agreement is separate; refund **90 000 of 150 000** is **casus-only** — universal terms only from **signed booking contract**.

### ЕИСЖС / dom.rf
- Public search for **project declaration** by developer/project (Scout signal https://www.domrf.ru/ — portal of housing construction ecosystem; declarations published in ЕИСЖС).
