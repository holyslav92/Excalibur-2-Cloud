## Legal framework — 214-FZ (current law, NOT future bill)

Source: https://base.garant.ru/12138267/5633a92d35b966c2ba2f1e859e7bdd69/ (214-FZ art. 5, accessed 2026-09-05)

- **Art. 5(3):** Price paid after state registration of DDU — lump sum or installments per contract schedule, cashless.
- **Art. 5(4):** Lump-sum payment: delay >2 months → developer may unilaterally terminate per art. 9.
- **Art. 5(5):** Installment schedule: systematic breach (>3 times in 12 months OR delay >2 months) → developer may unilaterally terminate per art. 9.
- **Art. 5(6):** Buyer late payment → penalty 1/300 of CBR refinancing rate per day on overdue amount.
- **Art. 5(2):** Price may change only if contract explicitly allows cases/conditions.
- **CBR FAQ** https://www.cbr.ru/analytics/finansirovanie-dolevogo-stroitelstva/questions/2020-02-20/ — installment with escrow allowed; payments per DDU after registration; no legislative ban on installment + escrow.

## Escrow vs pre-DDU payments

- **ENCO official** https://enco.ru/purchase/installment/ (Tyumen developer, accessed 2026-09-05): interest-free installment up to 1 year; for DDU, payments only after Rosreestr registration; DDU funds go to account specified in contract (escrow mechanism per law); "other contracts" may allow payment immediately after signing — distinction matters.
- **Novostroikino 06.08.2026:** All DDU installment tranches must go to escrow; direct transfers to developer for "internal installment" without DDU = gray scheme, no FRT protection.
- **Booking (pre-DDU):** Novostroikino 06.08.2026 — booking 3–30 days typical; payment as **advance** vs **non-refundable service** determines return; ZPP art. 32 for advance; no unified law on booking; penalty 8–15% in scout scenario is **contractual range**, not statutory — label as typical market clause, not guaranteed.

## Installment vs mortgage vs DDU stages

1. Booking agreement (optional) — fixes price temporarily; NOT 214-FZ protected; payments often to developer/agent.
2. Preliminary agreement / installment schedule before DDU — may exist; scout casus: payments under installment **before** DDU registration.
3. DDU registration — 214-FZ applies; escrow mandatory for new projects since 01.07.2019.
4. Installment under DDU — tranches to escrow; early payoff rules = **contract only** (current law has no mandatory no-fee early closure for developer installment).
5. Transition installment→mortgage before DDU — requires contract clauses; bank approval separate.

## Typical penalty/commission clauses (market, not law)

- Early closure fee / commission (% of balance) — contractual; scout model 8–15% of remainder.
- "Discount burns" if early payoff — price recalculated to list without promotion.
- Price increase on early termination — must check art. 5(2) DDU and booking/preliminary docs.
- **FZ on installment services (BNPL) from 01.04.2026** (RBC quote article) — bans commission for early payoff for **installment service operators**, NOT developer direct installment contracts.

## Return of payments on refusal/termination

- **Before DDU:** depends on booking/preliminary/installment contract — advance vs service; may withhold as penalty per contract.
- **After DDU registered:** termination by buyer without developer breach — generally difficult; mutual agreement or court; money on escrow returned per art. 15.5 214-FZ on termination; developer may claim penalties per contract/art. 5(6).
- **Developer unilateral termination** for buyer payment breach: 30-day warning required before termination (per dvitex/legal commentary on art. 5 + art. 9).
